# Medical disclaimer

[Español](./DISCLAIMER.md) · **English** · [Deutsch](./DISCLAIMER.de.md)

## Purpose

Define the scope of PreParto from a health-care point of view and state the limits of the app’s responsibility.

---

# Scope

PreParto is a support tool during late pregnancy and the start of labour.

Its goal is to help the user log information, understand symptoms better, and receive recommendations based on rules defined in advance.

The app does not replace professional health care.

---

# Not a medical device

PreParto is not a medical device.

It does not diagnose.

It does not prescribe treatments.

It does not confirm that labour has started.

It does not replace the judgement of doctors, midwives, or other health professionals.

---

# Recommendations

Recommendations generated by the app are informational.

The final decision on any action always belongs to the user and the health professionals who care for them.

---

# Emergencies

If there is a serious symptom, a worsening of health, or a situation that causes concern, the user should contact the appropriate health services immediately.

The app must not be used as a substitute for urgent medical assessment.

---

# Information provided

Recommendations depend on the information entered by the user.

Incomplete or incorrect information can produce recommendations that do not reflect the real situation.

---

# Content updates

The project’s rules and recommendations may be reviewed and updated as scientific evidence evolves or as the clinical guidelines used as a reference change.

---

# Closing principle

PreParto’s priority is to offer a safe, transparent, evidence-informed support tool, always complementary to professional health care.
