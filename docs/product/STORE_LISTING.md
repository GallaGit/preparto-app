## English

**Name:** PreParto

**Subtitle (placeholder):** Log contractions and late-pregnancy symptoms

**Short description:** A support tool to log contractions, symptoms, and a hospital bag list. It does not diagnose or replace clinical care.

**Long description (placeholder):**

PreParto helps you log contractions, late-pregnancy symptoms, and what you want to pack for hospital. Guidance follows documented rules and is not a diagnosis.

- Contraction timer and advisory 5-1-1 pattern

- Emergency screen to call 112 or the hospital number you save

PreParto is **not a medical device**. It does not confirm that labour has started and does not replace your care team. If you are unsure or feel unwell, contact health professionals.
